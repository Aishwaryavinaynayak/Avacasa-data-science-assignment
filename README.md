# Property Market Opportunity Analysis

## Overview

This project was completed as part of the Avacasa Data Science Internship assignment.

The objective was to organize and analyze real estate listings across three verticals:

• Resale  
• Long Term Rental  
• Short Term Rental  

The analysis focuses on comparing property pricing, rental performance, asset classes and micro markets to identify potential property opportunities.

## Analysis Performed

The analysis included:

• Cleaning and organizing the raw property listing data
• Standardizing asset classes and BHK configurations
• Classifying listings by micro market
• Classifying listings into resale, long term rental and short term rental verticals
• Analyzing resale price per square foot
• Comparing long term rental prices
• Analyzing short term rental performance
• Calculating implied gross rental yield
• Comparing performance across micro markets and asset classes
• Identifying areas that may be more suitable for rental positioning versus resale

## Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard was created to present the key findings in a sales friendly format.

The dashboard includes:

• Total listings
• Resale listings
• Long term rental listings
• Short term rental listings
• Median resale PPSF
• Resale pricing by micro market and asset class
• Long term rental performance by micro market
• Implied gross rental yield by micro market
• Comparison of resale pricing and rental yield

The dashboard is intended to help the sales team quickly compare markets and identify potential opportunities.

## Key Business Question

The analysis aims to answer:

**Which micro market and asset class currently shows the strongest case for short term rental positioning compared with a straight resale strategy, and where is the available data too limited to make a reliable recommendation?**

## Important Data Considerations

Some fields are blank because certain metrics are only applicable to specific property verticals.

For example, resale PPSF is not available for every rental listing, while short term rental metrics are not available for every resale listing.

Therefore, missing values were retained where they represented unavailable or non applicable information rather than being treated as zero.
